Full Compensation Plan

Grow your withdrawal balance through daily activity, check-ins, and your network. Fund plans from your deposit balance to unlock higher daily ad caps. All limits use Asia/Manila time. See Forfeited tasks & Catch-up at the bottom of this poster.

1. Trailer ad rewards (primary income)

Open Media, watch a trailer for about 10 seconds, then skip to credit your wallet. Each trailer counts once per day.

  • Pay per view = plan daily display income ÷ daily ad cap (credits to withdrawal wallet).
  • Higher subscription = more ads per day and higher earning potential.
  • +10 Ads Points per completed ad (redeem coming soon).

4. Agent program & referral commissions

Elite Pro and above may apply to become an Agent. After HQ approval, your referral link goes live — new sign-ups must use an approved Agent’s code.

When someone in your network buys or upgrades a plan, you earn on the amount charged (not deposit top-ups alone):

5% L1 Agent Commission
3% L2 Referral Gift
1% L3 Referral Gift

My Referrals stays locked until you’re approved · Apply from Profile → My Referrals

5. Forfeited tasks & Catch-up feature

Forfeited tasks: If you don’t complete your full daily ad cap (Manila day), the unfinished display income is added to your forfeited balance — nothing is thrown away; it waits for you in the app.

Catch-up feature: After you finish today’s regular ads, open Media and run catch-up trailer ads to withdraw from that saved balance.

  • Up to 3 catch-up ads per Manila day (fewer if your forfeited balance is smaller).
  • Same ₱ per view as regular ads; wallet credits like normal ad rewards.
  • Track progress on Task and Media — each catch-up also earns +10 Ads Points.
